About this project

This project recreates the excellent US Energy Flow Super Sankey by the Otherlab team in 2018 but incorporates heavy design inspiration from the Sankey diagram in Data: The First 50 Years by the Columnar team. The original stated goal was to "develop an energy display system which, in less than an hour, could give an extremely busy person an understanding of the size and the complexity of our national energy dilemma."

You can read more about my personal motivation to recreate this Sankey diagram in my newsletter.
