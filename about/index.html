About

Welcome to my little corner of the web! My name is Srini Kadamati and I've been based in the Boston area since 2018.

My mission is to make data easier to use. Data is crucial to understanding systems, but the tools for accessing, working with, and reasoning about data are still in their infancy.

My expertise spans technical education, developer advocacy, data science, product marketing, and product management. I developed this generalist skill set through working at startups across the data ecosystem.
